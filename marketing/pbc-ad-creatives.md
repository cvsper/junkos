## Copy rules (so every variant stays on-brand)
- Lead with the **pain or the outcome**, never "We are a junk removal company."
- Always name **Palm Beach** (local intent + relevance score).
- Always state the **3 advantages**: upfront price, same-day, you don't lift it.
- One emoji max in the first line; none in the headline.
- End on the **offer**. Every time.

## What to watch (from the playbook)
- D1–4: hands off (learning). Just confirm events fire + spend paces.
- D5: kill any creative with CTR < ~1% or zero InitiateCheckout.
- D7: scale the winner 20–30%, or swap the losing *angle* (keep structure).
- Judge on **cost per booking vs. job value**, not CTR.

## North star
First paid booking → fulfilled by your hauler → **5-star review + the real
before/after photo**. That photo replaces Creative 1's stock shot and becomes
your best-performing ad. The first loop funds the flywheel.
