  directory that sits idle.
- Prioritize **Miami (11 leads) and Fort Lauderdale (6)** — that's where
  your supply density is, and both have live SEO landing pages.
- Track replies. Add a `replied` / `interested` column to the lead CSV so
  Email 3 only hits people who went quiet.

  got the Apple Sign-In account-matching fix, so onboarding should work
