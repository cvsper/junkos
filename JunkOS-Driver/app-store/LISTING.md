## Description
Umuve Pro is the operator app for haulers on the Umuve network. Go online, get matched to junk-removal jobs near you, and run your whole route from one screen.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Go online and get dispatched to nearby jobs in real time
• See the job, photos, and pay before you accept
• Navigate to the pickup and share your ETA with the customer
• Capture before & after photos on site
• Track your earnings and get paid through Stripe
• Manage your truck, availability, and payout settings

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sign up and get approved as an Umuve operator
2. Set up payouts with Stripe Connect
3. Tap Go Online — nearby jobs come straight to your phone
4. Accept, haul, photograph, and get paid

Umuve handles the customers, booking, and payments so you can focus on the work. Built for independent haulers and junk-removal crews.

Umuve Pro is for approved operators. Junk-removal services are arranged and paid outside the App Store; the app is a tool for managing those real-world jobs.
