South Florida Recycling & Donation Guide

At Umuve, we believe that landfilling should always be the last resort. Whether you're handling a renovation or just clearing out a garage, knowing where your items go matters. This guide provides the most up-to-date drop-off locations and donation centers across Palm Beach and Broward counties.

Palm Beach County Resources

Solid Waste Authority (SWA)

The SWA operates multiple Home Chemical and Recycling Centers for hazardous waste, electronics, and bulk items.

  • North County: 14185 N. Military Trail, Jupiter, FL
  • Central County: 6161 Benoist Farms Rd, West Palm Beach, FL
  • South County: 1901 SW 4th Ave, Delray Beach, FL

Broward County Resources

Broward County Residential Drop-off Centers

Open to residents for bulk trash, electronics, and household hazardous waste.

  • North: 2780 N. Powerline Rd, Pompano Beach, FL
  • Central: 5490 Reese Rd, Davie, FL
  • South: 5601 W. Hallandale Beach Blvd, West Hollywood, FL
