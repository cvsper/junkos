Our Pricing Philosophy

Three things we refuse to do — because our competitors do them constantly.

No On-Site Estimate Requirement

We give you a real price estimate online before you book. You should never have to commit to a visit just to find out what something costs.

No Hidden Fees

The price includes labor, truck, fuel, and disposal. We do not add a "fuel surcharge" after the fact or charge extra for items that were visible when we quoted.

Pricing FAQs

How much does junk removal cost in South Florida?

The average junk removal job in South Florida costs $189 with Umuve. Prices range from $89 for single-item pickup to $3,500+ for full estate cleanouts. Your price depends on volume, item types, and access. Get an instant estimate online before booking.

Does the price include labor and disposal?

Yes. All Umuve pricing is all-inclusive — loading labor, truck, fuel, and eco-friendly disposal are included. There are no surprise add-ons when we arrive.

How does Umuve pricing compare to 1-800-GOT-JUNK?

Umuve is typically 25-35% lower than 1-800-GOT-JUNK. GOT-JUNK's minimum is $298+ for most jobs. Umuve starts at $89. For a half-truck load, Umuve typically costs $100-200 less than the same job with GOT-JUNK.

Do prices vary by city in South Florida?

Prices are largely consistent across all 42 South Florida cities we serve. Minor variations can occur based on drive distance to disposal facilities, local demand, and same-day surcharges during peak periods.

Is there a discount for large jobs?

Volume pricing naturally decreases per cubic yard as load size increases. Commercial accounts and recurring customers receive additional negotiated rates. Ask about our commercial pricing for businesses.

What is the cheapest way to get junk removed in South Florida?

Book a single-item pickup at $89 for one item. For larger loads, Umuve is typically the lowest-priced fully insured, eco-friendly option in South Florida. Local cash haulers may quote less but typically lack insurance and eco-friendly disposal practices.
