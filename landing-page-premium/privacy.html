1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

When you use our Services, you may provide us with the following information:

  • Name — your first and last name, provided during account creation or booking
  • Email address — used for account login, booking confirmations, receipts, and service communications
  • Phone number — used for SMS updates about your booking, driver arrival notifications, and customer support
  • Service address — the pickup location where junk removal will be performed
  • Photos — images you optionally upload of items to be removed, used to generate accurate quotes
  • Payment information — credit card, debit card, or Apple Pay details processed securely through Stripe (we never store your full card number)

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website or use our apps, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information — device type, operating system, browser type, and version
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, and referring URLs
  • IP address — used for approximate location, security, and analytics
  • Location data — GPS data from drivers during active jobs for real-time tracking; approximate location from customers for service area verification
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see Section 5 for details

Operator (Driver) Location — Umuve Pro App

The Umuve Pro operator app collects an operator's precise location to match the operator to nearby jobs, provide turn-by-turn navigation, and share a live ETA with the customer. With the operator's permission, this location is collected continuously and in the background — that is, while the app is not in the foreground — but only while the operator is online or has an active job. Background location collection stops when the operator goes offline and ends when the job is complete; this GPS data is deleted after job completion and is not stored permanently (see Section 6). Operators can disable location access at any time in their device settings, though doing so prevents the app from receiving and completing jobs.

Information from Third Parties

  • Apple Sign In — if you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email (we never access your Apple ID password)
  • Google Sign In — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile photo

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate our platform. These providers only access your data as necessary to perform their functions and are contractually obligated to protect it.

Stripe (Payment Processing)

All payment transactions are processed by Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. We never store your full credit card number on our servers. Stripe receives your payment details to process charges and issue refunds.

Twilio (SMS Communications)

Twilio powers our SMS notifications. Your phone number is shared with Twilio to deliver booking confirmations, operator arrival alerts, and other service-related text messages. Standard messaging rates may apply.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics collects anonymized data about page views, session duration, traffic sources, and user demographics. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Meta Pixel (Facebook)

We use the Meta Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram, and to show relevant ads to people who have visited our website. The pixel collects data about website visits, pages viewed, and actions taken. You can manage your ad preferences in your Facebook Ad Settings.

Google Ads

We use Google Ads conversion tracking to measure the effectiveness of our search advertising. This may involve cookies that track whether you completed a booking after clicking an ad.

Cloud Hosting

Our platform is hosted on cloud infrastructure providers that maintain industry-standard security certifications. All data is stored in the United States.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and gather usage data.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — required for core website functionality (e.g., maintaining your session during booking)
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use our site (Google Analytics)
  • Advertising cookies — used to measure ad effectiveness and deliver relevant ads (Meta Pixel, Google Ads)

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. However, disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website. You can also use the following tools:

  • Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on
  • Facebook Ad Preferences
  • Google Ads Settings

Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Our website does not currently respond to DNT signals, as there is no industry-standard protocol for compliance. However, you can use the opt-out tools listed above to manage tracking preferences.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account data — retained while your account is active, deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Booking and job history — retained for 3 years for dispute resolution, quality assurance, and record-keeping
  • GPS location data — deleted after job completion; not stored permanently
  • Payment records — retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations and tax law
  • Support correspondence — retained for 2 years after last interaction
  • Analytics data — retained in anonymized form for up to 26 months (per Google Analytics default settings)
  • Marketing preferences — retained until you opt out or delete your account

7. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, including:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Payment processing is handled by PCI-DSS compliant Stripe
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis
  • We use secure cloud infrastructure with regular security audits
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text

While we strive to protect your information using commercially reasonable measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
