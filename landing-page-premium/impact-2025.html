How we divert waste

  • 01

    Donate first

    Furniture, appliances, and household goods in usable shape go to local charities and community organizations whenever possible — so they get a second home instead of a spot in the landfill.

  • 02

    Recycle what we can

    Metal, cardboard, and e-waste are routed to recycling facilities. Electronics in particular are handled carefully so materials like lead and mercury are kept out of the ground and water.

  • 03

    Dispose responsibly

    Only what can't be donated or recycled goes to a permitted disposal site — sorted properly, never dumped illegally. It's the last resort, not the first move.

The South Florida context

Palm Beach and Broward generate a steady stream of bulky waste — moves, renovations, estate cleanouts, storm cleanups. A lot of it is recoverable, but only if someone takes the time to sort it before it's hauled off. That sorting is the part most haulers skip. It's the part we build into every job.
