Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn referral credits? +
Once you sign up as a partner, you receive a unique referral link. Every time a client books a job through your link — or you book on their behalf — you earn 10% of the job total as a referral credit. Credits are paid out monthly via direct deposit or check. There's no cap on what you can earn.
Can I book on behalf of my client? +
Absolutely. As a registered partner, you can book jobs directly on behalf of your clients. Just provide the property address and client contact info, and we handle everything from there. Your client gets VIP service and you earn your referral credit automatically.
What areas do you cover? +
Umuve provides same-day junk removal across all of South Florida, including Miami-Dade County, Broward County, and Palm Beach County. We cover cities from Homestead to Jupiter and everywhere in between.
Is there a minimum number of referrals? +
No minimums, no quotas. Whether you refer one client a year or ten a month, you earn 10% on every completed job. There are no tiers to maintain and no referrals expire. The program is completely free to join.
