FAQ

Common questions.

How much does junk removal cost?

We give an upfront, binding quote from the photos you send — the price you’re quoted is the price you pay. Pricing is volume-based; labor, hauling, and disposal are included. No hidden fees.

What items do you take?

Almost everything — furniture, appliances, electronics, yard debris, and construction waste. We can’t accept hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, or asbestos.

Do I need to be home?

Nope. Leave a key or arrange access and we’ll handle it — then text you before-and-after photos when the job’s done.

How fast can you pick up?

Most pickups happen same-day or next-day. Get an instant quote in the app and we’ll book your earliest slot.

What happens to my stuff?

We donate and recycle first — about 90% of what we collect is kept out of landfills, and usable items go to local charities.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Umuve operators are licensed and insured in Florida, and your property is protected on every job.
