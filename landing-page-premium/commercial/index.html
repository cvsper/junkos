Commercial Service FAQs

Do you offer commercial junk removal services?

Yes. Umuve specializes in commercial junk removal across Palm Beach, Broward, and Miami-Dade counties. Services include office cleanouts, retail store cleanouts, restaurant cleanouts, warehouse cleanouts, construction debris removal, property management cleanouts, and HOA bulk waste services.

Are your commercial services insured?

Yes. All Umuve operators carry commercial general liability insurance and workers compensation. We provide insurance documentation upon request for corporate and property management clients.

Can you schedule after-hours or weekend commercial cleanouts?

Yes. After-hours (evenings after 6 PM) and weekend scheduling is available for all commercial services. Most office and retail cleanouts are scheduled after business hours to minimize disruption.

Do you offer recurring commercial contracts?

Yes. Monthly and quarterly contracts are available for property managers, HOAs, contractors, and businesses with ongoing junk removal needs. Volume discounts apply.

How do commercial prices compare to residential?

Commercial services are priced differently due to volume, scheduling complexity, insurance requirements, and documentation standards. Commercial jobs typically start at $299 and scale with the size of the project. Call for a custom quote.
