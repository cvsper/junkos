Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle eviction cleanouts?

Yes. We handle eviction cleanouts sensitively and efficiently. We document all removed items with photos, which can be important for legal proceedings. We do not enter units without proper authorization.

How fast can you turn over a unit?

Most one-bedroom apartments can be cleared in 1-2 hours. We offer same-day service for property managers and can often accommodate morning requests the same day.

Do you offer monthly billing for multiple properties?

Yes. Property management companies with regular volume can set up monthly billing accounts with discounted rates. Contact us to discuss your portfolio size.

What documentation do you provide for abandoned property?

We provide photo documentation before removal, an itemized list of removed items, and a completion certificate. This supports tenant dispute resolution and legal compliance.

Can you handle emergency same-day cleanouts?

Yes. Property managers with urgent turnover needs can call (561) 944-1636 for priority dispatch. We keep same-day slots reserved for commercial accounts.
