Everything between "I can't park in there" and "I can."

Questions

What people ask before they book.

How much does a garage cleanout cost in Palm Beach County?

We price by how much of the truck you fill, with the 8% service fee included: $193 for the minimum pickup, $301 for a quarter load (about one wall of shelving), $420 for a half load (a one-car garage's worth) and $625 for a full load (a packed two-car garage). Same-day adds 25%, next-day 10%, weekends 15%. The exact number is shown before you pay.

What can't you take from a garage?

Paint, propane tanks, batteries and tires can come along with a small per-item disposal fee set by our pricing engine: $10 per can of paint, $5 per propane tank, $5 per battery, $5–20 per tire depending on size. We can't take chemicals, solvents, pesticides, pool acid or anything else classed as hazardous waste — Palm Beach County's Solid Waste Authority accepts those free at its home chemical drop-off.

Do I need to sort or bag anything first?

No. Point at what goes and the crew does the rest, including boxes that have gone soft from the humidity. If you want tools, bikes or sports gear donated rather than dumped, set them aside and say so; the crew keeps them separate.

How long does a garage cleanout take?

A quarter load is usually under an hour. A full two-car garage is typically two to three hours with a two-person crew, including the sweep.

Can you do it while I'm at work?

Yes, if the garage can be opened for us — a keypad code, a neighbor, or you unlocking the side door. You get before-and-after photos by text, and payment only settles once the job is marked complete.

What if my garage is more than one truckload?

Text a photo to (844) 435-6005 and we'll price the whole job in minutes. Multi-load garages are quoted as one number up front, not load by load on the day.
