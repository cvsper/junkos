Questions

What office managers ask before they book.

How much does an office cleanout cost in Palm Beach County?

One-time office cleanouts are priced by the truckload, all-in with our 8% service fee: about $193 for an eighth of a truck, $301 for a quarter, $420 for a half, $528 for three-quarters and $625 for a full truck on a weekday with two or more days' notice. Same-day adds 25%, next-day 10%, weekends 15%. Business accounts use a separate flat rate card: $445 per quarter load, $795 per half, $995 per three-quarter and $1,195 per full load with disposal included up to two tons.

Can you work after hours or on a weekend?

Yes. Weekend pickups carry a 15% surcharge on one-time bookings; evening slots after the building's normal hours are arranged when you book. Tell us the loading-dock hours and whether the freight elevator needs a reservation so the crew shows up when the building will let them in.

Do you provide a certificate of insurance for our building?

Yes. Every crew on Umuve is a licensed, insured operator whose documents we verify before they can take jobs. If your property manager needs a certificate of insurance on file before the truck arrives, ask when you book and we'll send it ahead.

What happens to the furniture and electronics?

Furniture in usable condition goes to donation partners first where they'll accept it; the rest goes to a licensed transfer station where metals are separated for recycling. Computers, monitors and printers are handled through our electronics recycling service. We don't wipe drives — remove or destroy them before pickup, or keep them.

Do you shred documents?

No. We don't offer certified document destruction. Shred sensitive paper with a shredding vendor first; sealed boxes of non-sensitive paper ride out with the rest of the load as general junk.

How do business accounts work?

Sign a short commercial agreement and every cleanout is billed from the same rate card with one consolidated monthly invoice. Each job gets a binding written quote from photos or a site visit, before-and-after photo documentation, and next-business-day scheduling with two-hour arrival windows. Three or more cleanouts in a calendar month earn 10% off that month's invoices. Start at goumuve.com/partners or portal.goumuve.com/signup.
