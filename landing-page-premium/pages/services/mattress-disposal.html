Questions

What people ask before they book.

How much does mattress disposal cost in Palm Beach County?

$127 all-in for a mattress of any size anywhere in Palm Beach, Broward or Miami-Dade: $99 removal, a $20 recycling fee and our 8% service fee. A mattress and box spring together is $243. A full bed set (mattress, box spring and frame) is $161 because the set price already covers recycling. Same-day pickup adds 25%, next-day 10%, weekends 15%, and no job is under $119.

Why is there a recycling fee on mattresses?

Transfer stations in South Florida charge per mattress and box spring because springs jam compactors and the foam takes landfill space out of proportion to its weight. We pass that through at $20 per piece instead of hiding it in the base price. Bed frames and full bed sets don't carry it.

Can I just put my mattress on the curb?

In most Palm Beach and Broward cities you can, on a scheduled bulk-pickup day — but the rules, wait times and wrapping requirements vary by city and HOA, and some condo and apartment buildings don't allow it at all. If you'd rather not have a mattress on the curb in August for a week, or your building won't let you, we'll take it from the bedroom the same day.

Do you take mattresses with bed bugs?

Yes — tell us when you book. We bring it out sealed in a mattress bag so nothing rides along in the truck or your hallway, and it goes straight to disposal. Please don't try to donate or resell a mattress that has had bed bugs.

Can I donate my old mattress instead?

Rarely. Nearly every charity and thrift store in South Florida declines used mattresses for hygiene reasons, even clean ones. Bed frames and headboards in good shape are a different story and can often be donated or sold.

Do I need to carry it downstairs or outside first?

No. The price includes carry-out from wherever the bed is — upstairs bedroom, condo hallway, garage. Clear a path to the door and we do the rest. A queen mattress weighs 70–100 pounds and a king more; that's the part you're paying us for.
