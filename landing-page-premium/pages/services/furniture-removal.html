Where it goes

Donation first — honestly.

Most people would rather see a good sofa get a second home than a landfill, and so would we. But South Florida charities are strict, and the humidity has already made a lot of decisions for you. Here is what actually happens.

Usually accepted for reuse

  • ✓Solid-wood tables, dressers and bookcases with all the parts
  • ✓Upholstery that's clean, dry and unripped — no pet odor, no sagging
  • ✓Office chairs and desks in working order
  • ✓Patio sets that aren't rusted through

Goes straight to disposal

  • –Stained, torn or pet-damaged upholstery of any kind
  • –Anything with signs of bed bugs — we bag it before it moves through your building
  • –Particleboard that's swollen or delaminated from humidity or a leak
  • –Sleeper sofas and recliners with broken mechanisms
  • –Mattresses in almost every case; they're recycled where facilities accept them

The price is the same either way. Tell us the condition when you book and the crew will know what to bring — and what not to promise.

Before we arrive

Four things that make it go fast.

The building

Book the elevator. Most Palm Beach and Broward condos want a reserved window and pads on the elevator. Do that with management, then put the window in your booking notes.

The pieces

Empty them. Drawers out or taped shut, cushions on, nothing inside the sleeper. Anything you want to keep, move it out of the room so it's not in the path.

The path

Clear a lane. Rugs rolled, plants moved, the front door propped. A sectional needs a clear turn at every corner.

Tell us

Stairs, narrow doors, condition. Flights of stairs, a door under 32 inches, water damage or bed bugs — in the notes, so the right crew and gear arrive the first time.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

How much does furniture removal cost in Palm Beach County?

We price by the piece and show the total before you book: a sofa is $119, a sectional $169, a recliner or dresser $99, a dining table $99, a coffee table $59, plus an 8% service fee. Every job has a $119 minimum, so a single small piece is $119 all-in. Four or more pieces get 10% off, eight or more 15%, sixteen or more 20%. Same-day pickup adds 25%, next-day 10%, weekends 15%.

Do you carry furniture down from upper floors or condos?

Yes. A two-person crew carries everything out by hand — elevators, stairwells, parking garages. Stairs beyond the ground floor are $15 per flight. If your building requires an elevator reservation or a loading-dock window, book that with management first and tell us the time; we'll match it.

Will you donate my furniture instead of dumping it?

When a piece is in resale condition, our crews route it to donation or reuse first. Be realistic about what qualifies: most South Florida charities refuse stained, torn or pet-damaged upholstery, sleeper sofas with damaged mechanisms, particleboard that has swollen from humidity, and anything with signs of bed bugs. Those go to a licensed transfer station.

Do I need to take the furniture apart first?

No. If a bed frame, desk or wall unit has to come apart to fit through a door, we do it for $25 per item — just tick "needs disassembly" when you book so the crew brings the tools. Sectionals separate into their pieces at no charge.

Can you pick up a single sofa today?

Usually, yes. Same-day pickup adds 25% to the item price, so a single sofa is $161 all-in today, $129 with two days' notice. Book online and you'll see the slots open for your address.

What if the furniture is wet, moldy or has bed bugs?

Tell us when you book. We still take it — it just skips donation and goes straight to disposal, and the crew bags or wraps upholstery with bed-bug evidence before it goes through your building. Water-logged sofas and mattresses weigh two to three times their dry weight; that's normal after a South Florida storm and doesn't change the price.
