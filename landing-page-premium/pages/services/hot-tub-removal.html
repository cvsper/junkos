Before we arrive

Four things that make it go fast.

The day before

Drain it. Open the drain valve or siphon with a garden hose to the lawn or storm drain. A dry shell is lighter and cuts cleaner.

Power

Breaker off. Plug-in tubs: unplug. Hard-wired 220V: an electrician disconnects at the spa panel first. Our crews don't open panels.

The path

Clear the route. Move planters, grills and furniture between the tub and the gate. Unlock the gate; put dogs inside.

Tell us

Narrow gates, stairs, screen rooms. Add it to the booking notes so the right crew and saws show up the first time.

Questions

What people ask before they book.

How much does hot tub removal cost in Palm Beach County?

$593 all-in for a standard tub (up to 7 ft, 4–6 seats) anywhere in Palm Beach, Broward or Miami-Dade — a $549 base plus our 8% service fee, with cut-up, carry-out, hauling and disposal included. Large tubs (7–9 ft, 6–8 seats, or on a raised deck) are $699 base, $755 all-in. Same-day adds 25%, next-day 10%, weekends 15%. Typical local quotes run $400–600 before disposal and cut-up fees for a standard tub, and $700–1,200 for large ones.

Do I need to drain the hot tub first?

Yes, the day before. A full tub holds 300–500 gallons and takes hours to empty. Open the drain valve or siphon with a garden hose, then leave the cover off so the shell dries — it's lighter to cut and carry.

Do you disconnect the electrical?

Turn the breaker off before we arrive. Plug-in (110V) tubs just get unplugged. Hard-wired (220V) tubs need a licensed electrician to disconnect at the spa panel first — we'll tell you exactly what to ask for. Our crews don't open electrical panels.

How do you get it out of a fenced backyard?

We cut the shell into three or four sections so each piece clears a standard 36-inch gate, then carry them out by hand. No crane, no fence removal. Mention narrow gates, stairs or a screened enclosure when you book so we bring the right crew.

Can you remove an in-ground spa or a swim spa?

In-ground and gunite spas are demolition, not removal, so they're quoted individually. Same for swim spas over 10 ft and tubs on a raised deck that has to come apart. Text a photo to (844) 435-6005 and you'll have a number in minutes.

What happens to the hot tub after you take it?

It goes to a licensed transfer station. Where local facilities accept them, the wood frame, pump and heater metals and the cover are separated for recycling rather than going straight to landfill.
