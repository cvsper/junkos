Do you wipe hard drives before recycling? +
Yes. We offer secure data destruction for all storage devices. Hard drives can be wiped using DoD-standard methods or physically destroyed. Data destruction certificates are available upon request — essential for Palm City businesses handling sensitive data.
Is it illegal to throw away electronics in Florida? +
Yes. Florida prohibits TVs, computers, and certain electronics from regular household trash due to hazardous materials like lead, mercury, and cadmium. Martin County enforces these regulations. Umuve ensures full compliance for Palm City residents and businesses.
