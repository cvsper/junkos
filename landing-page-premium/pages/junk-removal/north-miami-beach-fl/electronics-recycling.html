Do you wipe hard drives before recycling? +
Yes. We offer secure data destruction for all storage devices. Hard drives can be wiped using DoD-standard methods or physically destroyed. Data destruction certificates are available upon request — essential for North Miami Beach businesses handling sensitive data.
Is it illegal to throw away electronics in Florida? +
Yes. Florida prohibits TVs, computers, and certain electronics from regular household trash due to hazardous materials like lead, mercury, and cadmium. Miami-Dade County enforces these regulations. Umuve ensures full compliance for North Miami Beach residents and businesses.
