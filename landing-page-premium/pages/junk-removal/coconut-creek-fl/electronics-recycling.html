Do you wipe hard drives before recycling? +
Yes. We offer secure data destruction for all storage devices. Hard drives can be wiped using DoD-standard methods or physically destroyed. Data destruction certificates are available upon request — essential for Coconut Creek businesses handling sensitive data.
Is it illegal to throw away electronics in Florida? +
Yes. Florida prohibits TVs, computers, and certain electronics from regular household trash due to hazardous materials like lead, mercury, and cadmium. Broward County enforces these regulations. Umuve ensures full compliance for Coconut Creek residents and businesses.

Coconut Creek Disposal Rules to Know
