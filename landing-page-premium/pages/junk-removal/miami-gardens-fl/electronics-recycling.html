Is it illegal to throw away electronics in Florida? +
Yes. Florida prohibits TVs, computers, and certain electronics from regular household trash due to hazardous materials like lead, mercury, and cadmium. Miami-Dade County enforces these regulations. Umuve ensures full compliance for Miami Gardens residents and businesses.
