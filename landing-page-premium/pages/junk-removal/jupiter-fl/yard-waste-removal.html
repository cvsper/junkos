Jupiter Disposal Rules to Know

Palm Beach County Solid Waste Authority handles residential bulk pickup (2 items per week max). Electronics are banned from regular trash and must go to certified e-waste facilities. Hazardous waste goes to the PBC SWA facility at 6890 N Jog Rd. Jupiter Farms properties may fall under unincorporated Palm Beach County rules with different bulk pickup schedules.
