Frequently Asked Questions — Appliance Removal in Miami Beach

How much does appliance removal cost in Miami Beach? +
Appliance removal in Miami Beach starts at $119 for a single appliance. A washer/dryer pair is typically $139. Full kitchen appliance removal averages $299. All prices include disconnection and eco-friendly disposal.
Do you disconnect appliances in Miami Beach? +
Yes. Our operators can disconnect most standard appliances including washers, dryers, refrigerators, and dishwashers. For gas appliance disconnection or complex electrical work, we recommend hiring a licensed plumber or electrician first. We serve all Miami Beach neighborhoods.
Do you handle refrigerant removal from appliances? +
Absolutely. All refrigerant-containing appliances (refrigerators, AC units, dehumidifiers) are transported to EPA-certified facilities where refrigerants are properly recovered per Section 608 guidelines. Full compliance guaranteed in Miami-Dade County.
What happens to old appliances after removal? +
95% of appliance materials are recycled. Metals (steel, copper, aluminum) are melted down, plastics are processed, and refrigerants are recovered. We use certified recycling facilities throughout Miami-Dade County. Usable appliances are donated to Habitat for Humanity of Greater Miami.
