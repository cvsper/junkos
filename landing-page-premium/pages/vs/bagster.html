Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Bagster really cost?

The Bagster bag itself costs approximately $30 at Home Depot. Waste Management charges $300-400+ for pickup, depending on your location in South Florida. Total typical cost: $330-430. This does not include any overweight surcharges if you exceed the weight limit.

Does Umuve cost more or less than Bagster?

In most cases, Umuve costs less than Bagster when you account for both the bag purchase and pickup fee. Umuve pricing for a comparable load starts around $149-250, and our crew does all the loading. You pay more at Bagster to do the work yourself.

How much can a Bagster hold versus an Umuve truck?

A Bagster holds 3 cubic yards — roughly the equivalent of a standard pickup truck bed. Umuve trucks hold 10-13 cubic yards. If you have more than a small renovation's worth of debris, Umuve can handle it in a single visit.

Can Bagster take heavy materials like concrete?

Bagster has weight limits (typically 3,300 lbs for concrete/dirt). Exceeding limits may result in the bag being left behind or additional charges. Umuve is priced by volume and handles heavy debris including concrete and tile regularly.

Will a Bagster sitting in my driveway violate my HOA rules?

Possibly. Many South Florida HOAs prohibit construction materials, bags, and bins in driveways for more than 24-48 hours. Umuve arrives, loads, and leaves the same visit — no HOA issues.
