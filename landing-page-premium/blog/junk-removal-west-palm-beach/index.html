The West Palm Beach Waste Ecosystem

In West Palm Beach, waste management is a two-tiered system. You have the municipal services provided by the City and the broader infrastructure of the Solid Waste Authority (SWA) of Palm Beach County. While the City handles standard trash and limited bulk pickup, they aren't designed for the "heavy lifting" or the "on-demand" needs of most residents and businesses.

When Municipal Services Aren't Enough

If you've ever tried to leave an old sectional sofa on the curb in Flamingo Park only to have it sit through three rainstorms because it "didn't meet the requirements," you know the frustration. City bulk pickup has strict rules on pile size and placement. If you have a pile larger than a few cubic yards, or if you need it gone on a Tuesday but your day is Friday, you're out of luck.
