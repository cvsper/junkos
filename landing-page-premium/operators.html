Operator FAQ

What do I need to start?

A truck or van, a valid driver's license, basic liability coverage, and a smartphone. If you're still getting set up, apply anyway — we'll point you in the right direction.

Do I need to find my own customers?

No. That's the whole point. Umuve brings the customers and books the jobs — you just accept the ones you want and haul.

How much can I make?

It depends on how many jobs you accept. You keep the majority of every completed job, and there's steady demand across Palm Beach and Broward. You control your schedule and your volume.

When do I get paid?

Payouts run through Stripe. Standard payouts are free on a regular schedule, and you can cash out the same day for a small fee when you want the money faster.

What areas can I work?

We're active across Palm Beach and Broward County right now and expanding. Apply and we'll let you know about jobs in your area.

How do I get the app?

Once you're approved, we'll send you the link to the Umuve Pro operator app and help you get online.
