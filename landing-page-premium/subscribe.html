Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes. All Umuve subscriptions are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. Cancel anytime before your next billing cycle. If you're on an annual plan, you'll receive a prorated refund for unused months.
What happens to unused pickups?+
Unused pickups roll over to the next month. Starter plans roll over up to 1 pickup, Professional plans roll over up to 2, and Enterprise plans have unlimited rollover. Rollovers expire after one billing cycle — use them or lose them.
Can I add extra pickups beyond my plan?+
Absolutely. Additional pickups are available at a discounted subscriber rate — $99/pickup for Starter, $89 for Professional, and $79 for Enterprise. That's well below our standard one-off pricing of $175+.
What areas do you cover?+
Umuve subscription service covers all of South Florida — Palm Beach County, Broward County, and Miami-Dade County. That's 42+ cities from Jupiter to Homestead. If you manage properties across multiple counties, we've got you covered.
How does the dedicated operator work?+
Professional and Enterprise subscribers are assigned a dedicated operator who learns your properties — access codes, preferred pickup spots, item types, scheduling preferences. They handle all your pickups for consistency, and you get their direct line for quick scheduling changes. If they're unavailable, a vetted backup handles your pickup with full notes.
